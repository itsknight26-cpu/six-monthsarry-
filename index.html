For my wifey,

This little book is for you — for the random conversations, the silly moments, the dreams we share, and all the love that keeps growing between two people miles apart.

“Distance can't make these moments mean less.”

chapter one · our beginning

I'm glad our paths crossed.

Out of all the people in this big world, somehow our paths found each other. And from that beginning came someone I now get to call my wifey.

Thank you for letting me into your days and for making room for me in your world. I'm grateful for the story we're writing, one conversation at a time.

02
✧
chapter two · our little world

From random talks to big dreams.

One of my favorite things about us is how we can talk about almost anything. A random topic can turn into a whole conversation about our future — our wedding, having kids someday, and the little home and life we sometimes imagine.

I love that we can dream out loud together. Those conversations are little reminders of the possibilities we're excited about.

chapter three · six little chapters

Every month, a new page.

One — the beginning of our story.

Two — getting to know each other more.

Three — finding joy in our everyday conversations.

Four — learning, growing, and understanding.

Five — making more memories across the distance.

Six — still here, still grateful, still us.

04
❦
chapter four · even on difficult days

I still want us.

I know we won't always agree. Sometimes we'll misunderstand each other, get upset, or need time to figure out what we're feeling. But an argument doesn't erase the love I have for you.

Even on those days, I still want to understand you, work through things with you, and keep building something healthy together. I don't need us to be perfect. I hope we keep choosing patience, honesty, and kindness.

05
✧
chapter five · my favorite future

Someday, a little closer.

Sometimes I think about the day when we won't have to say goodnight through a screen. I imagine ordinary days together — sharing meals, laughing over silly things, making plans, and turning the dreams we talk about into memories.

We don't have to rush any of it. I want us to grow at our own pace and build our future one thoughtful step at a time.

a little reminder for you

Whenever you miss me.

Come back to these pages and remember that somewhere, even from miles away, there's someone who is grateful to have you in their life.

I hope you never forget how much you matter to me, wifey. No matter how ordinary the day may be, you are still one of my favorite parts of it.

“If I could choose again, I'd still choose to know you.”

chapter six · my letter to you

Happy sixth monthsary, wifey.

Six months may be just one little chapter in a much bigger story, but I'm so thankful for every part of it. Thank you for the random topics, the laughs, the sweet moments, and even the conversations where we imagine what our future could look like.

I love you always. Not because every day is easy, but because what we have matters to me. I want to keep learning how to love you with care, respect, and understanding.
